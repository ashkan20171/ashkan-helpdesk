# Phase 2.84 — AI Executive Intelligence & Decision Center

## Overview
Phase 2.84 adds an executive decision layer that unifies operational, financial, AI and governance signals into a leadership-ready workspace.

## Frontend capabilities
- Executive readiness score and business pulse
- Revenue, service health, AI efficiency and forecast-confidence KPIs
- Strategic portfolio view across revenue, operations, AI economics and governance
- AI-assisted executive recommendation sequence
- Decision register with search and status filtering
- Confidence visualization and decision detail modal
- Human-oversight and governance promotion notes
- Responsive enterprise UI
- New route: `/ai-executive`
- New sidebar item: **AI Executive Intelligence**

## Navigation integrity
The phase also restores the complete frontend route catalog from the feature modules already present in the project, including authentication modes, core HelpDesk modules, workplace modules and all AI intelligence modules.

## Backend-ready follow-ups
- Persisted executive snapshots and decision records
- Real KPI/event aggregation from ASP.NET Core APIs
- Role/tenant-aware executive views
- Decision approval workflow and audit evidence
- Forecast and scenario model integration
- Scheduled executive brief generation
- Export to PDF/PowerPoint and secure sharing
